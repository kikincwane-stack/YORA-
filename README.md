# YORA 💜🦋
### Your Words. Your World.

A little corner of the world where every feeling has a voice.
Write freely. Feel deeply. Let your words live.

**Live App:** https://yora-poetry-community--kikincwane.replit.app

---

## 🌸 What is YORA?

YORA is a poetry community website where anyone can share their poems,
read poems from around the world, and spread love to other writers.

No judgement. Just words. Just feelings.

## ✨ What You Can Do

- **Create an account** - Sign up with your email to join YORA
- **Write a poem** - Give it a title and let your feelings become words
- **Discover poems** - Read fresh poems from poets everywhere, newest first
- **Show love** - Tap ❤️ Love on poems that touch your soul
- **Explore feelings** - Love, Heartbreak, Healing, Life, Dark Poetry, Self Discovery

## 🛠️ Built With

- HTML - Structure
- CSS - The purple beauty 💜
- JavaScript - Makes it work
- Firebase - Login + Saving poems in real time
